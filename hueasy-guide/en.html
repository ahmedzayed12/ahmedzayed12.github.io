What can you do with Hueasy?

Automatic rewriting and one-click download

Upload a Word document and start processing. Hueasy rewrites it automatically and makes the new Word file available to download in one click.

Supports .docx files up to 20 MB.

Open Hueasy

Compare original and rewritten paragraphs

When comparisons are available, review the two versions side by side or download the comparison as HTML.

Rewrite pasted text

When enabled in the English and Turkish workspace, paste up to 100,000 characters. Copy the result, download it as plain text or formatted HTML, or use the Word template when available.

Copy text from a processed Word file

After processing a document in the English and Turkish workspace, you can copy the resulting text directly from the interface.

Subscription access

Choose a one-, three- or six-month subscription. English and Turkish share an access code. You can request Arabic activation on the same code.

Single-document trial

If a trial code is available, you can process one English or Arabic Word document of up to 1,000 words. The code is used after successful processing.

After downloading your document

From rewriting to reviewing your Turnitin report

Hueasy rewrites your document and helps you review changes. If your institution uses Turnitin, refer to its AI Writing Report, when available, and its Similarity Report for the submitted version.

Review Turnitin’s AI Writing Report (when available) and Similarity Report to assess the final document against your institution’s requirements.

Review the wording and citations

Compare the rewritten paragraphs with the originals and check that the meaning, citations and references are accurate.

Submit through your institution

Download the final Word file and submit it through the Turnitin workflow specified by your university or assessor.

For researchers and LaTeX users

Download your document as a LaTeX package

After processing a Word file in the English workspace, select Prepare complete LaTeX (.zip) and then Download complete LaTeX (.zip).

Extract the archive and open main.tex. Keep the bundled image and font folders alongside it. The package supports compatible tables, equations, links, footnotes and images. Review the page layout in your LaTeX project.

Frequently asked questions

Which file format should I upload?

Choose a Word .docx document up to 20 MB. If your file is .doc, save a .docx copy first.

Where does the rewritten file appear?

The download button appears in the workspace when processing finishes. Your device keeps the original, and Hueasy downloads the result as a separate copy.

How do I activate the Arabic workspace?

Contact Hueasy to request Arabic access on your existing subscription code.

Can I export LaTeX from the Arabic workspace?

The LaTeX package option appears in the English workspace. You can download rewritten Arabic documents as Word files.

Is there a universal acceptable similarity score?

Check your institution’s threshold and settings. Requirements can vary by course or publisher. Review matched passages and citations alongside the overall score.

Is the Similarity Report the same as the AI writing indicator?

The Similarity Report shows matches with sources. The AI writing indicator, where available, is reported separately and interpreted according to your institution’s policy.

How do I get an access code?

Contact the Hueasy team to request a code and learn about subscriptions.

Ready to rewrite your document?

Contact Hueasy to request an access code or ask about subscriptions.
