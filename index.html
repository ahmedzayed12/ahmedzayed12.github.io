AI Fingerprint
V8.8.5

Not a one-score AI detector. AI Fingerprint is an evidence architecture that combines textual inference with native DOCX/PDF forensics, cryptographic checks, provenance, reconstruction and auditable reporting.

● V8.8.5 · CONSENSUS ARBITRATION

From “AI score” to an evidence case.

The system deliberately separates calibrated model probabilities from uncalibrated forensic indices, limits the authority of any single detector, prevents correlated evidence from being counted twice, and records findings with their source, validation method, strength, manipulability, interpretation and alternative explanation.

Scientific research software

AquaDyn 3D
Research Studio v12

An independent computational hydraulics and morphodynamics research environment combining the established 2D shallow-water workflow with an advanced volumetric 3D CFD workspace and research-grade visualization.

● v12 · ADVANCED 3D RESEARCH STUDIO

Hydraulics, scour and free-surface flow in one research workspace.

AquaDyn is designed for experimental and academic workflows: define geometry and boundary conditions, run hydraulic and morphodynamic models, examine bed evolution and local scour, inspect velocity and free-surface fields in 3D, compare engineering formulas with numerical results, validate configurations, and export data for independent post-processing.

Hydraulics & scour intelligence

  • HEC-18 pier-scour formulation with live correction factors.
  • CSU / comparison methods plus Melville–Coleman and Breusers-style analysis.
  • Abutment and contraction scour support, bed shear and sediment mobility fields.
  • Horseshoe-vortex proxy analytics, stagnation pressure and downflow indicators.
  • Froude zoning, energy-grade-line output and hydraulic performance diagnostics.

Research workflow & quality control

  • Safe / Enhanced / Pro simulation presets.
  • Dedicated Setup, Physics, Numerics, Run, Results, Report, Validation, Experiments, Mesh and 3D CFD workspaces where applicable.
  • Mesh-quality reporting, sensitivity / UQ panel and numerical verification smoke tests.
  • Probe-based measurement, checkpoint/restart, CSV/structured data and VTK-oriented export paths.
  • Interactive camera, water opacity, bed/scour overlays, obstacles, streamlines and velocity cones.
